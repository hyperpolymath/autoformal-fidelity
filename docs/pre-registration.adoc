= autoformal-fidelity — pre-registration (2026-10-12)

An *application* of the type family, not a family. Question: can the
family give a disciplined view on whether a formal artefact F says what
its informal source S says (the autoformalisation fidelity problem)?

== Honest limit, stated first

Semantic fidelity is not decidable in general. The claim here is narrower:
the *structure* of an unfaithful crossing can be made unconstructible, and
the residue of belief can be made honest about being belief.

== Pre-registered next statements (each with its falsifier)

. *Additions need the target standpoint.* Extend `Standpoints` so the
  target reading may take values outside the image of `fwd`; show
  additions are exactly the worlds the source side cannot distinguish
  from each other but the target can. Falsifier: an addition visible from
  the source standpoint.
. *Stage accounting is K-CUT-LOSS.* A pipeline of crossings graded in
  resource-algebra's `Loss` (`keep`/`residue`/`forget` per element) and
  cut at successive points; the loss accounted on the whole pipeline
  restricted to a standpoint equals the loss read locally up to the
  projected cut. Falsifier: a per-stage audit whose sum disagrees with
  the global accounting.
. *Verdicts are warrants, not knowledge.* The fidelity verdict as an
  epistemic-types `Warrant`; the structural checks (F-1..F-3) are its
  type-determined, sound part; "this statement means that claim" has no
  `sound` field. Falsifier: a structural check that admits an unfaithful
  crossing.
. *Provenance by dependence.* Each verdict names the source passages it
  rests on (residual-evidence `Dependent`); an erratum retracts exactly
  the verdicts resting on it. Falsifier: a verdict unaffected by
  retracting its own support.
. *Multiple provers.* Two formalisations as two target standpoints;
  agreement is identical partitions; convergence is evidence only under
  independence — stated, not certified.

== Relation to the estate

* Round trip = injectivity, additions = empty fibres: the same notion the
  Harvard dehallucinator is built around (an output with no fibre in the
  evidence).
* First application to draw on four families at once (echo loss,
  epistemic warrant, residual dependence, choreographic cut).
